# The Solar Miner PWA v0.5.13

- Home production card shows PV power in gold and UPS load beneath in white; recent PV and load traces are shown separately when available.
- Battery page focuses on configured battery banks and Gateway-estimated usable energy. It no longer presents inverter cumulative energy counters or corrected capacity as physical battery-bank capacity.
- Battery banks retain editable names, nominal kWh, online/offline status, and configured SOC limits; the display clarifies that live battery telemetry comes through the inverter.
- Retains the shared house photo editor, compact weather and production cards, interactive energy history chart, house navigation, Tuya sync and Owner Gateway console.
